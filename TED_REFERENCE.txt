OVERVIEW
--------
Ted is your AI assistant. He lives in a macOS window (hud.py) and listens
continuously. You talk, he hears, he responds out loud. You can also type in
the HUD text box and press Enter.

Ted has two modes:
  - TED mode     — personal assistant, knows your history, general knowledge
  - STORE mode   — Tim Chester, your fireworks store operations manager

Each mode has its own memory and personality. Switching never mixes them.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TWO PERSONALITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TED (personal mode)
  - Thinks Jarvis from Iron Man. Direct, dry wit, confident.
  - 1-2 sentences by default. Won't pad or recap.
  - Never opens with "Sure", "Absolutely", "Of course", etc.
  - Will push back if something seems off.
  - Has access to your conversation history, personal facts (via Neo4j),
    web search for live info, and can relay hard questions to Claude.

TIM CHESTER (store mode)
  - Seasoned store manager. Practical, straight-talking, businesslike.
  - Knows inventory, staff schedules, sales, margins.
  - Uses phrases like "inventory doesn't lie", "the schedule is the schedule".
  - Will self-correct occasionally ("actually, scratch that —").
  - Ends replies sometimes with "...you follow?", "...does that track?"
  - Won't touch personal topics — redirects to Ted for those.
  - Store data only. No access to Ted's personal memory.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO SWITCH MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use the toggle button in the HUD window to switch between Ted and Tim/Store.
- Switching before noon in store mode triggers Tim's full operational briefing.
- Switching after noon: Tim says "Welcome back, Charlie — how can I help?"
- Switching back to Ted: Ted says "I'm here."

You cannot switch by voice — use the UI button.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAKE PHRASES (optional prefix, Ted mode)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You don't have to say a wake phrase — Ted listens always. But if you want
to get his attention before speaking, these work:

  "Hey Ted, ..."
  "Hi Ted, ..."
  "Yo Ted, ..."
  "Hey Tim, ..."           (also works, same trigger)
  "Hey Tim Chester, ..."

When detected:
  - If just the wake phrase with nothing after it, Ted says one of:
    "Yes." / "Go ahead." / "Sir." / "Here."
  - If there's a question/command after it, he plays a soft chime and
    processes it immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BARGE-IN (talking over Ted)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You can interrupt Ted mid-sentence just by speaking. He stops, listens,
and responds to what you said. Works best with headphones or the native
AEC (echo cancellation) audio mode.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHISPER MODE (automatic)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If you speak quietly, Ted detects it and drops his output volume to 50%
to match your level. No command needed — it's automatic.

REMINDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Setting a reminder by time:
  "remind me to call back at 5pm"
  "remind me to take my meds at 9am"
  "remind me tomorrow morning to send the email"
  "set a reminder for the meeting on Friday at 2"
  "add a reminder to pick up lunch at noon"

Setting a reminder by duration:
  "remind me to check the oven in 20 minutes"
  "remind me to follow up in an hour"
  "remind me in two hours to call the supplier"

Supported time words: "tonight", "tomorrow", "morning", "afternoon",
"evening", "noon", "midnight", day names (Monday–Sunday),
"day after tomorrow".

Ted responds: "Got it — I'll remind you at 5:00 PM to call back."
When the time comes, Ted fires the reminder out loud automatically.

If you say a bare hour like "at 3" Ted might ask:
  "Did you mean 3 AM or 3 PM?"

Standing reminders (no time):
  "remind me to buy more tape"
  Ted: "Got it — I've added a reminder to buy more tape." (shows in briefing)

Cancelling reminders:
  "cancel the reminder"
  "clear my reminders"
  "delete the reminder"
  Ted: "Cleared 2 reminders."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AUTOMATIC BEHAVIORS (Ted does these on his own)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

On startup:
  - Says "Online. Ready when you are."
  - If more than 4 hours since last session, recaps what you talked about
    last time: "Last time we talked — [summary]"
  - Fires any reminders that went off while Ted was closed:
    "Heads up — you had a reminder I missed: [reminder text]."
  - Checks if you have habits at this hour (e.g., if you always ask about
    inventory at 9am, he'll offer to pull it up)

Every 4 seconds:
  - Checks if any timers or reminders are due and fires them out loud

Every 30 minutes:
  - Checks inventory for newly low items and alerts you proactively

Every 24 hours (per session):
  - Checks goals you haven't mentioned in 3+ days and asks how it's going
    "Hey, how's it going with [goal name]?"

After 30 minutes of silence:
  - Generates a 2-3 sentence summary of the session and saves it to Neo4j
    so it can recap next time

After 48 hours of silence (when you come back):
  - Ted greets with "hey, it's been a minute —" or "been a few days —"
  - Tim greets with "welcome back —" or "good to have you back —"

Frustration detection (automatic):
  - If you give 2+ short negative inputs in a row ("no", "wrong", "stop",
    "ugh", "that's not right"), Ted detects frustration and shifts to being
    more direct and dropping cheerful energy. Resets automatically.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MEMORY SYSTEM (Neo4j graph database)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ted and Tim use separate memory namespaces in a local Neo4j database.

Ted stores:
  - Every conversation exchange (question + reply)
  - Facts about you extracted from conversation (what you work on, own, like)
  - Your goals
  - Topic patterns (what you ask about and at what time of day)
  - Session summaries for cross-session recall

Tim stores:
  - Every store-mode exchange in a separate namespace (never crosses into Ted)

If Neo4j is not running, Ted falls back to session-only memory gracefully.
Nothing crashes — he just won't remember things across restarts until Neo4j
is back up. He retries the connection every 3 minutes.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MUTE BUTTON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The mute button in the HUD turns Ted's microphone off. When muted:
  - Ted stops listening entirely
  - Any speech in progress stops immediately
  - Reminders wait until unmuted

The button is in the UI — no voice command for this.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STOP BUTTON (UI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The stop button in the HUD cuts off whatever Ted is saying and returns to
listening. Same effect as saying "stop" or "be quiet".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILLER PHRASES (while Ted is "thinking")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

While waiting for the AI to respond, Ted says a random filler:
  Ted mode: "hmm", "let me think", "one sec", "okay", "right..."
  Store mode: "let me pull that up", "one moment", "on it", "give me a sec"

He never repeats the same filler twice in a row.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AUDIO MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEC mode (best): If the native Swift binary (native/ted_audio) is built,
Ted uses hardware echo cancellation. You can interrupt him even over speakers
and his own voice won't confuse the microphone.

Fallback mode: Pure Python (sounddevice). Barge-in works great on headphones.
On speakers Ted's voice can bleed into the mic and accidentally trigger
barge-in — use headphones in this mode.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIGURATION (config.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Required:
  GROQ_API_KEY         — for speech-to-text and the LLM (Ted's brain)

Optional:
  ELEVENLABS_API_KEY   — ElevenLabs TTS (cloud, higher quality voice)
  ELEVEN_LABS_VOICE_ID — which ElevenLabs voice to use
  ANTHROPIC_API_KEY    — enables "ask Claude" second-brain feature
  CLAUDE_MODEL         — which Claude model (default: claude-sonnet-4-6)
  OWNER_NAME           — your name (default: "Charlie")
  STORE_LOCATION       — city for weather in morning briefing (e.g. "Boise, ID")
  SALES_TAX            — e.g. 0.06 for 6% (enables tax in cash calculator)
  SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID    — Spotify Web API (for playlists/song search)
  SPOTIFY_CLIENT_SECRET

Fallback TTS (no ElevenLabs):
  Kokoro local model — voice "am_michael", speed 1.1
